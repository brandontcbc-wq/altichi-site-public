01 — How I Work

A Clear Path From
First Call to Handoff.

Every engagement, regardless of domain, follows the same eight-phase path, so scope, ownership, and expectations are never a guessing game.

1

Initial Discussion & Statement of Work

A short conversation about the constraint, followed by a written scope, deliverables, and timeline.

2

Kickoff

Align on stakeholders, access, communication cadence, and success criteria before any work begins.

3

Discovery & Blueprinting

Understand the current system, constraints, and requirements in enough depth to design against them.

4

Architecture & High-Level Design

Design the target state and the trade-offs, reviewed together before anything gets built.

5

Project Charter & Scope Confirmation

Formalize what's in scope and what's out, so the build phase has no ambiguity to work around.

6

MVP Build

Implement, automate, and test against the confirmed scope, with regular checkpoints along the way.

7

UAT

Validate the build against real use cases before anything is called done.

8

Documentation, Training & Handoff

Full documentation, hands-on training, and a clean transition of ownership to your team.
