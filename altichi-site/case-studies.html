Cloud-Native Microservices & CI/CD Platform
Problem A monolithic application needed to be decomposed into independently deployable services with automated delivery pipelines.
Containerized services deployed to Amazon ECS with Fargate using ARM64 Graviton for cost-optimized serverless compute. Implemented fully automated CI/CD via AWS CodePipeline and CodeDeploy with a blue/green deployment strategy, backed by Amazon RDS MySQL in private subnets. A monolithic EC2 deployment was considered and rejected for limited scalability and tight component coupling. Single-AZ RDS was chosen over Multi-AZ to control cost within project scope, a deliberate trade-off rather than an oversight.

Simulated Attack Chain Case Studies

Three simulated AWS attack chains: privilege escalation via Lambda, privilege escalation via EC2 instance profiles, and a full SSRF to breach simulation modeled on the 2019 Capital One incident. The common thread is dangerous permission combinations, not single misconfigurations, being what actually gets exploited.

FEATURED CASE STUDY
S3 Data Exfiltration Breach Simulation
Problem Demonstrate how a real-world attack pattern chains SSRF, exposed instance metadata, and an over-permissioned IAM role into a full data breach.
Provisioned a vulnerable AWS environment with Terraform (17 resources: VPC, EC2 reverse proxy, IAM role, S3 bucket) modeled on the 2019 Capital One incident. Exploited a Host-header SSRF vulnerability to reach the EC2 Instance Metadata Service, extracted temporary IAM credentials, and used them to enumerate and exfiltrate data from a restricted S3 bucket. Documented full root-cause analysis and defensive recommendations, including enforcing IMDSv2.

MLXGBoost
Binary Classification: Churn & Fraud Detection
Applied the same supervised-learning and imbalance-handling lens to two different rare-outcome business problems: telecom customer churn (roughly 26.5% positive class) and credit card fraud (roughly 0.17% positive class). Compared Logistic Regression and XGBoost across class weighting, SMOTE, undersampling, and threshold tuning for both. For fraud specifically, threshold-tuned XGBoost was selected as the practical business-balanced model: recall improved from 75.5% to 85.7% (missed fraud cases dropped from 12 to 7), trading precision down to 71.2% (false positives rose from 1 to 17), a deliberate, quantified trade-off rather than an unqualified win.
